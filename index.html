🧮 Pharmacology Math

Dosage Formula

D ÷ H × Q = X

D = Desired Dose

H = Dose on Hand

Q = Quantity

X = Amount to Administer

💊 Introduction to Pharmacology

What is Pharmacology?

Pharmacology is the study of medications, how they work, how they are used, and how they affect the body.

Drug Names

  • Chemical Name – Scientific name.
  • Generic Name – Official medication name.
  • Brand Name – Manufacturer's trade name.

Drug Effects

  • Therapeutic Effect – Intended benefit.
  • Side Effect – Expected but unwanted effect.
  • Adverse Effect – Harmful or unexpected reaction.
  • Allergic Reaction – Immune response to a medication.

💉 Administering Medication

Medication Safety

  • Verify the medication order.
  • Confirm the patient's identity.
  • Check for allergies.
  • Use the correct route.
  • Document administration promptly.

Injection Angles

Route Angle
Intradermal (ID) 5–15°
Subcutaneous (SubQ) 45° or 90°
Intramuscular (IM) 90°

⭐ High-Yield Facts

  • Use the D ÷ H × Q formula for dosage calculations.
  • 1000 mg = 1 g.
  • 1000 mL = 1 L.
  • Know the common medication routes and injection angles.
  • Always follow the rights of medication administration.
