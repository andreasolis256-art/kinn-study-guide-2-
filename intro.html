What is Pharmacology?

Pharmacology is the study of medications, including how drugs work, how they are used, and how they affect the body.

What is a Drug?

A drug is a substance used to:

  • Prevent disease
  • Diagnose conditions
  • Treat illness
  • Manage symptoms

Drug Names

Chemical Name

The scientific description of a medication's chemical structure.

Generic Name

The official name of the medication.

Example: Ibuprofen

Brand Name

The name given by the manufacturer.

Example: Advil or Motrin

Drug Effects

✅ Therapeutic Effect

The intended or desired effect of a medication.

⚠️ Side Effect

An expected effect that may be unwanted.

🚨 Adverse Effect

An unexpected or harmful reaction.

❗ Allergic Reaction

An immune response to a medication. Symptoms may include rash, itching, swelling, or difficulty breathing.

Medication Routes

  • PO: By mouth (oral)
  • SL: Sublingual (under the tongue)
  • Topical: Applied to skin
  • Transdermal: Absorbed through skin patch
  • IM: Intramuscular injection
  • SubQ: Subcutaneous injection
  • ID: Intradermal injection

Controlled Substance Schedules

Schedule Description
I No accepted medical use and high abuse potential
II High abuse potential but accepted medical use
III Moderate dependence potential
IV Lower abuse potential
V Lowest controlled substance category

⭐ Test Tips

  • Know the difference between generic and brand names.
  • Know therapeutic vs side vs adverse effects.
  • Memorize common medication routes.
  • Remember controlled substances are regulated by the DEA.
